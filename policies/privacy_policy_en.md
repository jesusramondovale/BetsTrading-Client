
# Privacy Policy – Use of Camera in the App

This application respects and protects the privacy of all users. We are fully committed to ensuring that any use of sensitive features, such as the device's camera, is done with transparency and only for essential and explicitly authorized purposes.

## When and Why We Use the Camera

The **camera will only be used in two specific scenarios**, both initiated by the user:

1. **Profile Picture Upload**  
   Users may voluntarily choose to take a photo using their camera to set or update their profile picture within the application. This action is entirely optional and not required to use the app’s core features.

2. **Identity Verification (Age Confirmation)**  
   To comply with legal requirements and ensure that all users are of legal age, users may choose to take a photo of their identity document (DNI/passport) to verify their identity and age. This process is optional unless required for specific features.

## Privacy and Data Usage

- Your personal data, identity documents, and any images captured are never shared with third parties.
- All personal data, operations, and balances within the app are processed **anonymously** and securely.
- The company **reserves the exclusive right to commercialize anonymous data** for analytical or business purposes, never disclosing any personal or identifiable information.

By using this application, you agree to this policy.
